What We Cover

Personal coverage,
explained in plain English

A closer look at the core pieces of a well-built personal insurance program. Every household is different — this is where we start the conversation.

Homeowners

Homeowners Insurance

Your home is likely your largest asset — the right policy protects the structure, your belongings, and you, if someone is ever injured on your property.

  • Dwelling, other structures, and personal property coverage built to true replacement cost
  • Personal liability and medical payments protection for guests and accidents on your property
  • Loss-of-use coverage so you're taken care of if you're ever displaced from your home
  • Optional endorsements for water backup, service line, and equipment breakdown

Auto

Primary Personal Auto

Liability, collision, and comprehensive coverage for your everyday vehicles — shopped across carriers so you're not overpaying for the coverage you actually need.

  • Bodily injury and property damage liability limits built to protect your assets, not just meet the minimum
  • Collision and comprehensive coverage for repair or replacement of your vehicle
  • Uninsured/underinsured motorist protection and rental reimbursement
  • Multi-vehicle and multi-policy bundling discounts across your household
Get started →
Umbrella

Personal Umbrella / Excess Liability

An affordable extra layer of liability protection that sits above your home and auto policies, guarding your savings and future earnings from a serious lawsuit or judgment.

  • Additional liability limits in $1M increments, layered over your existing policies
  • Coverage that follows you — auto accidents, property incidents, even some personal injury claims like libel or slander
  • Broader worldwide protection than most underlying policies provide on their own
  • Especially important for households with teen drivers, rental properties, pools, or higher net worth
Get started →
Jewelry & Valuables

Jewelry, Fine Art & Valuables

Standard homeowners policies cap what they'll pay for jewelry, watches, art, and collectibles. Scheduled personal property coverage insures these items at their real, appraised value.

  • Agreed or appraised value coverage for engagement rings, heirlooms, and fine jewelry
  • Coverage for fine art, wine, musical instruments, and collectibles
  • No deductible on most scheduled items, with worldwide coverage for loss, theft, or mysterious disappearance
  • We'll help you get items appraised and scheduled correctly from the start
Get started →
Landlord & Investment Property

Landlord & Investment Property Insurance

A standard homeowners policy isn't built for a property you rent out. Dwelling fire and landlord policies protect the structure, your rental income, and your liability as a landlord.

  • Dwelling and other-structures coverage built for a non-owner-occupied or rented property
  • Loss-of-rents coverage if the property becomes uninhabitable after a covered loss
  • Landlord liability protection for injuries or damage tied to the rental unit
  • Portfolio and multi-property programs for landlords with more than one investment property
Get started →
Household Employees

Workers' Comp & EPLI for Domestic Staff

If you employ a nanny, housekeeper, caregiver, or estate staff, you take on employment exposures that fall outside a standard homeowners policy. Household employee coverage closes that gap.

  • Workers' compensation coverage for injuries sustained by household employees on the job
  • Employment Practices Liability (EPLI) for claims of wrongful termination, harassment, or discrimination
  • Guidance on state-specific requirements for nannies, housekeepers, and estate staff
  • Protection that a standard homeowners liability policy typically excludes outright
Get started →
Life & Disability

Life Insurance & Disability Income

Life insurance protects your family if you're gone. Disability income insurance protects your family's finances if an illness or injury keeps you from working — a risk that's often overlooked.

  • Term and permanent life insurance sized to your family's income replacement needs
  • Short- and long-term disability income coverage to protect your paycheck
  • Coverage for self-employed individuals and business owners without an employer group plan
  • Policy reviews as your family, income, and mortgage change over time
