Specialized coverage for

A closer look at the industries and operations we work with most. Wherever your business fits, this is where we start the conversation.

Main Street Business

Main Street Small Business

Retail shops, offices, and service businesses need coverage that flexes with day-to-day operations — property, liability, and the ability to keep the doors open after a loss.

  • Business Owners Policy (BOP) combining property and general liability in one package
  • Commercial property coverage for your building, inventory, and equipment
  • Business interruption coverage to protect income if you're forced to close temporarily
  • General liability protection for customer injuries and property damage claims

Multifamily & HOA

Multifamily Living & HOA

Master policies for apartment communities, condominiums, and homeowner associations — built to protect the property, the board, and the residents who live there.

  • Master property and general liability coverage for common areas and shared structures
  • Directors & Officers (D&O) liability protecting board members from personal exposure
  • Fidelity and crime coverage for association funds handled by the board or manager
  • Umbrella/excess liability layered on top for added protection
Get started →
Professional & Mgmt Liability

Professional & Management Liability

Consulting firms, executives, and organizations with complex risk exposure need protection that goes beyond general liability — for the advice you give and the decisions you make.

  • Errors & Omissions (E&O) coverage for claims tied to professional advice or services
  • Directors & Officers (D&O) liability protecting leadership decisions
  • Employment Practices Liability (EPLI) for wrongful termination, harassment, or discrimination claims
  • Fiduciary Liability (FLI) for those managing employee benefit plans
Get started →
Construction

Trade Contractors & Construction

Contractors, subcontractors, and project owners face risk on every job site — from tools in transit to the finished structure itself.

  • General liability coverage for job-site injuries and property damage
  • Inland marine coverage for tools, equipment, and materials in transit or on-site
  • Builders risk coverage for structures under construction
  • Workers' compensation for crews, subcontractors, and day labor
Get started →
Freight & Transportation

Freight Transportation

Fleets of any size, on any route — trucking liability, cargo, and physical damage coverage built for the realities of the road.

  • Trucking liability coverage for bodily injury and property damage on the road
  • Motor cargo coverage for freight being hauled
  • Physical damage coverage for tractors and trailers
  • Owner-operator programs for independent drivers and small fleets
Get started →
Warehouse & Distribution

Warehouse & Distribution

Storage, fulfillment, and logistics operations carry unique exposures for goods that aren't even yours — coverage built around inventory in your care, custody, and control.

  • Bailee coverage for customer goods stored or handled on your premises
  • Inland marine coverage for goods in transit between locations
  • Commercial property coverage for the facility, racking, and equipment
  • Product liability coverage for goods that pass through your hands
Get started →
Restaurant & Hospitality

Restaurant & Hospitality

Restaurants, bars, and hotels face a mix of exposures most businesses don't — from serving alcohol to keeping the kitchen running through an equipment failure.

  • Liquor liability coverage for establishments that serve alcohol
  • Food spoilage coverage for loss of refrigerated inventory
  • Equipment breakdown coverage for kitchen and HVAC systems
  • Workers' compensation tailored for restaurant and hotel staffing
Get started →
Assisted Living & Nursing

Assisted Living & Nursing Care

Senior care facilities, memory care communities, and in-home care providers carry heightened liability exposure that calls for specialized coverage.

  • Professional liability coverage for care-related claims
  • General liability coverage for facility and visitor incidents
  • Abuse and molestation liability coverage
  • Workers' compensation for caregiving and support staff
Get started →
Key Person Life

Key Person Life Insurance

Business-owned coverage that helps protect against the financial loss of an executive or other essential employee — the person whose absence would hit the business hardest.

  • Business-owned life insurance on owners, executives, or essential staff
  • Proceeds that help cover lost revenue, recruiting, and transition costs
  • Often paired with buy-sell agreements for partnerships and closely held businesses
  • A frequent lender or investor requirement for business financing
